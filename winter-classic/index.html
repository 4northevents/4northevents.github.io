Format & rules

4Game guarantee
2Pool games
65Min pool · finish the batter
75Min bracket · finish the inning
DEDouble elimination bracket
ITBIf tied in bracket play
  • Bracket seeding is based on pool play results.
  • Higher seed is the home team in bracket play.
  • All players must be uploaded to your online USSSA roster before the tournament begins.
  • New protest rules: see page 64 of the USSSA Fastpitch rule book.
  • 8U: bunting is allowed, but no pull-back or slap swing (automatic out). A foul bunt on strike 3 is an out.
How USSSA breaks seeding ties
  1. Winning percentage (highest first)
  2. Number of wins (most first)
  3. Number of losses (fewest first)
  4. Head-to-head winning percentage (only if all tied teams played each other)
  5. Average runs allowed (lowest first)
  6. Average run differential, max 8 (highest first)
  7. Ranking points (highest first)
  8. Date the team entered the USSSA database

USSSA Fastpitch rules apply. The format isn't set in stone and may change as the event gets closer. Watch your USSSA app alerts for the latest.
